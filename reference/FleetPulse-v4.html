How to Calibrate (Once per Bike, Repeat Yearly)

  1. Be consistent: Always read voltage the same way — bike switched off and resting for a few minutes (voltage under load or right after charging reads higher).
  2. 100% point: After a full charge finishes and rests, note the voltage and update the 100% row.
  3. 0% / low points: Ride down and note the voltage when the bike shows empty/reserve (0%–10%). Never drain a lithium pack below its safe BMS cutoff.
  4. Middle points: Use a plug-in kWh meter. Charging from voltage A to full adds X kWh; X ÷ (Battery kWh ÷ Charger Efficiency) = the % missing at A.
  5. Monotonic curve: Keep voltages unique per bike and % strictly rising with voltage (the Check column warns you if inverted).

60-Second Tour & Workbook Methodology Guide

Complete reference from the source Excel workbook

1. The 60-Second Workflow

Configure your bikes once in Vehicles. Use the prominent + Log Entry button to record charges, petrol refills, or odometer readings in under 30 seconds. View verified full-to-full efficiency, non-cash battery wear, and EV vs Petrol savings on the Dashboard and Mileage & Expenses tabs.

2. Full-to-Full Mileage Method

Mileage is only calculated as Verified when a Full charge or Full tank closes a stretch that began at the previous Full fill. Partial charges or partial refills in between are accumulated into the closing full fill and never divided on their own. The previous full fill's own energy is excluded.

3. EV Km per Full Charge Formula

Km/Charge = Distance × (Battery kWh ÷ Charger Eff) ÷ Wall kWh Used. Topping up from 34% to 100% scales accurately to a 100% battery pack.

4. EV Battery Wear Estimator

Battery Price = Capacity × ₹/kWh. Distance Considered = min(Cycles × Avg Km/Charge, Years × 365 × Daily Km). Uses your all-time average valid km/charge (or Rated Km/Charge until a valid stretch is logged).

5. Maintenance & Service Retirement

Maintenance is tracked strictly separately from running cost. Logging a newer service record for the same vehicle and category automatically retires any older pending reminder in that category.
